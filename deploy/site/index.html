Why the server sits in Hong Kong

This is a platform for users worldwide.

🌏

Global reach, low latency

Hong Kong is a major internet exchange hub.

💳

A path to real payments

Integrating Stripe or PayPal requires an offshore compliant entity.

⚖️

Relatively open policy

Hong Kong is a free-market jurisdiction with clear commercial precedent.

🚫

Why not mainland China

Mainland restrictions on cross-border settlement and data export are severe.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers.

Can the market use real money right now?
No. We are in the simulated payment testing stage.
What happens if I top up?
The credits you top up now are test credits.
Why is the server in Hong Kong?
Three reasons.
Does Hong Kong hosting avoid regulatory requirements?
No. Hong Kong hosting is a commercial and technical choice.
Do I have to use the Hong Kong server?
No. The engine is MIT open source.
How much does the platform take?
It defaults to 10%.
Is the 90% payout a real accounting entry?
Yes. On acceptance the provider balance increases by 90%.
Do I need to register?
No for local AI chat. Registration is only needed to use the market.
Will my data go abroad?
It depends on which model you use.
