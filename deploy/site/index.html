Frequently asked questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to pay to use the engine?
No. The engine is MIT open source. You only pay for the model API you choose to connect.
Does the engine send my files to a third party?
It depends on which model you connect. Use a local model and nothing leaves your machine.
Do I have to use a hosted server?
Three reasons.
Is my data private?
Yes. Self-hosting means the data, the model keys and the deployment all live on your own machine.
Can I deploy it anywhere?
No. The engine is MIT open source.
Which models can I connect?
Any OpenAI-compatible API, plus local models such as Llama and Qwen.
Can I run it on my own server?
Yes. That is the recommended way to use it, and your data never leaves your server.
Do I need to register?
No. Local AI chat needs no registration at all.
Will my data go abroad?
It depends on which model you use.
