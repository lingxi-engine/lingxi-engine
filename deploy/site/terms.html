4. Market Rules

Task Bounty Market

  • When an employer posts a task, the stated budget is deducted from the employer's balance as a hold.
  • When a contractor's quote is accepted, only the contract amount is held. Any difference is returned to the employer.
  • On successful acceptance of a delivery, the contractor receives 90% of the contract amount. The platform retains 10% as a fee.
  • Cancelled tasks return the full budget to the employer at zero fee.

Agent Marketplace

  • Listings may be priced in credits, USD, or CNY units.
  • A price cap applies to bare agent listings to prevent abuse.
  • Purchase transfers funds: buyer is debited, seller is credited 90%, platform retains 10%.

General Conduct

  • No fraudulent listings, no fake delivery, no plagiarism, no impersonation.
  • No use of the platform for unlawful content, spam, or any activity that violates applicable law.
  • Disputes are resolved through the platform's arbitration flow: release, refund, or partial refund.

5. Disputes and Arbitration

Either party may open a dispute on a contract. The platform arbitrates based on delivery records, message history, and audit logs. Outcomes: release (pay contractor), refund (return to employer), or partial refund (split). Arbitration is final and binding within the platform's simulated currency scope.
